Custom Software Development Cost in India 2026

India 2026 cost ranges for MVPs, internal tools and multi-platform custom software, plus the scope decisions that change the quote.

There is no honest single price for custom software. There is a honest way to budget. Here is how JB Software Solutions frames 2026 India engagements for founders who need clarity before they sign.

Ballpark ranges (INR)

  • Marketing website with SEO foundations: roughly ₹50,000 to ₹5,00,000 depending on pages, CMS and integrations.
  • Web MVP / SaaS slice: ₹3,00,000 to ₹15,00,000 for a focused first release.
  • Mobile MVP (Flutter or React Native): ₹5,00,000 to ₹20,00,000.
  • Internal tool / CRM-style system: ₹4,00,000 to ₹25,00,000+ based on roles, workflows and integrations.

Premium or regulated builds (fintech, health, complex hardware) sit above these bands. Offshore hourly mid-market rates commonly land around $15 to $49 per hour depending on seniority.

What moves the number

  • Number of user roles and approval flows
  • Third-party integrations (payments, WhatsApp, ERPs)
  • Offline / desktop / hardware requirements
  • Compliance, audit logs and data residency
  • How finished the design and product decisions already are

How to not waste money

Freeze an MVP that proves one business outcome. Ship in milestones. Keep IP and source access in writing. Pair the build with basic SEO/AEO so launch day is not also day zero of discoverability.
