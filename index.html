How is JB different from other India software and digital agencies?

Many competitors sell the same menu: apps, websites, AI and SEO. We differentiate with founder-accessible delivery, desktop capability when needed, and discovery engineered into the product so launch day is not day zero of marketing.

A focused web MVP often lands in 2 to 6 weeks. Mobile MVPs typically take 6 to 12 weeks depending on integrations and store requirements.

Both. Engagements can be fixed-scope projects, dedicated pods, or ongoing retainers for SEO, GEO and product iteration.

Is the website indexed for search and AI crawlers?

Yes. Pages include canonical URLs, sitemap.xml, robots.txt, llms.txt, structured data and crawlable HTML so Google and AI systems can discover and cite our services.
