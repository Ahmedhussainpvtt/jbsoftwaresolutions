Who owns the code after a software project?

Clear FAQ on software IP ownership and handover with JB Software Solutions.

Who owns the code?

For client projects, you own the work we create for you under the contract.

What is handed over?

Source, key assets and docs needed to keep running or extend the product.

Are there lock-ins?

We do not hold your product hostage. Ownership and access are agreed in writing.

Do you keep a copy?

Working copies during delivery are normal. Commercial IP for client work is yours as contracted.
