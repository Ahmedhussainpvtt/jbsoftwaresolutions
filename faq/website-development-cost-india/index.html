FAQ

Website development cost in India

What business website development costs in India, what is included, and what changes the quote.

How much does website development cost in India?

At JB Software Solutions, business website development often starts from ₹50,000 for a focused multi-page site. Custom web apps and e-commerce cost more.

What is included in a website development package?

Page structure, mobile-friendly layout, contact form, basic SEO setup, speed checks and a launch checklist.

How long does a business website take?

Many 5 to 12 page sites finish in 2 to 6 weeks once content and approvals are ready.

Do you build SEO-friendly websites?

Yes. Clean URLs, sitemap, schema where useful, and pages written for buyer questions, not just decoration.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Yes. We audit speed, clarity and enquiry flow, then rebuild or improve without throwing away what already works.

Is WordPress okay for a business website?

Sometimes. We recommend WordPress when your team needs frequent editorial updates. For product-heavy sites we often prefer a modern web stack.

Do you write website content?

We can guide structure and headlines. Final business claims and legal copy stay with you unless content is scoped in.

What pages should a service business website include?

Home, services, about, proof or process, FAQ, pricing guidance if you can share it, and contact.

Will my website work on phones?

Yes. Mobile layout is part of every website development project.

Do you provide hosting?

We can recommend and set up hosting. You keep ownership of the domain and accounts.

What is the difference between a website and a landing page?

A website covers multiple offers and trust pages. A landing page focuses on one campaign or offer.

Can a website generate leads?

Yes, if offers are clear, forms are easy, and pages answer buyer questions. That is how we scope lead-generation websites.

Still unsure?

Send your goals and constraints. We reply with fit and next steps.
