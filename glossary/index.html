Glossary

Short, clear definitions of the words people use when they hire a software company in India.

Custom software development - Building software made for one business instead of buying a ready-made product.
Website development - Creating a business website that explains your offer and captures enquiries.
Web app development - Building a product that runs in the browser with login and workflows.
Mobile app development - Building apps for Android and iPhone phones and tablets.
Desktop app development - Software that installs on a computer when a website is not enough.
SEO - Search engine optimization: helping people find you on Google.
AEO - Answer engine optimization: showing up in short answer results.
GEO - Generative engine optimization: helping AI tools mention your brand.
Workflow automation - Connecting tools so repetitive work happens without copy-paste.
CRM software - A system to track leads, customers and follow-ups in one place.
ERP software - Software that connects core business processes like stock, orders and finance.
SaaS - Software as a service: a product customers use online on a subscription.
MVP - Minimum viable product: the smallest first version that proves value.
Fixed-scope project - A project with written deliverables, timeline and price before build starts.
Software IP ownership - Who owns the code and design after the project is done.
Lead generation website - A site built to turn visitors into enquiries, not just look pretty.
App store launch - Publishing an app on Google Play or the Apple App Store.
Internal tools - Software your own team uses to run the business day to day.
Page speed - How fast a website loads on phones and computers.
Conversion rate - The share of visitors who take a useful action, like sending a form.
Schema markup - Extra labels on a page that help search and AI systems understand it.
llms.txt - A text file that helps AI systems understand a website.
India software development company - A team in India that builds software for local and global clients.
Hire developers in India - Engaging an India team to build or improve your product.
WhatsApp business automation - Using rules and tools so WhatsApp messages trigger follow-ups automatically.
Google Business Profile - Your free business listing on Google Maps and local search.
Core Web Vitals - Google's main measures of how fast and stable a page feels.
Offline-first app - An app that still works when the internet is weak or missing.
Product discovery - The short planning phase before build, to agree goals and scope.
Retainer - A monthly engagement for ongoing work like SEO or product updates.
